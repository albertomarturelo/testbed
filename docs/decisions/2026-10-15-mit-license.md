# MIT license

## Status

Accepted — 2026-10-15 (from the founding brief)

## Context

casefile is meant for broad adoption in company repos.

## Decision

Release under MIT.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Apache-2.0** — patent grant, heavier notice requirements.
- **AGPL / source-available** — protects a future hosted offer (F4) but deters adoption.

## Consequences

- Anyone may embed or fork; F4 hosted must compete on service, not license.
